Study plan

Distinguishing Mandatory and Default Rules: Criteria and Consequences

Seven sessions of 60–90 minutes each, without an assumed deadline. Each session: 5 minutes of recall, 35–55 minutes of reading or writing, and 20–30 minutes of application and summary.

Day Focus Task Deliverable
1 Define the scope Read the introductory source and identify the terms. Definitions in your own words and a research question.
2 Directed reading [L2]: Read the opening discussion and analysis of binding force and conditions of application. A source card with section or page locator.
3 Compare evidence Complete the remaining readings; identify what each establishes. Three supported claims and their limits.
4 Apply Answer the exercise below before checking the guidance. Reasoned analysis separating assumption from fact.
5 Outline Assign evidence to sections and formulate a provisional answer. A one-page argument plan.
6 Write and compare Read the English report alongside the Arabic version; write your own version. A draft with clear citations.
7 Verify and rehearse Check sources and terminology; rehearse a five-minute explanation. Revised report and confident answers.

Reading order

  • [L2]: Read the opening discussion and analysis of binding force and conditions of application.
  • [L3]: Printed pages 63–66 on the classification criteria.
  • [L1]: Articles 230, 232, and 502; search within the PDF.

Exercise

Compare advance exclusion of liability for fraud with an agreement on a different delivery location. Why do the outcomes differ?

Answer guidance: Start with article 232 in the first case and article 502 in the second. Examine whether variation is permitted, then distinguish the clause’s effect from the fate of the whole contract.

Active recall

  • Explain the research question without opening the files.
  • What does each source support, and what does it not establish?
  • Explain the example and justify the outcome.
  • What still needs checking before submission?

When studying all five courses together, complete session 1 for each, then session 2, and so on. The whole programme involves about 35 sessions; it is not a requirement to complete five courses in seven days.

Report outline

Distinguishing Mandatory and Default Rules: Criteria and Consequences

Research question

How can mandatory and default rules be distinguished, and how does this distinction limit contractual freedom?

Structure

  1. Introduction: significance, research question, and method.
  2. Part I: definitions, textual criterion, and substantive criterion.
  3. Part II: contrary agreements and applications of articles 232 and 502.
  4. Conclusion: a qualified answer and limits of generalization.

Scope and length

The supplied report is a complete concise treatment, not a 1,500-word research paper. The photos do not specify a numerical word count. If your instructor requires 1,200–1,600 words, expand the evidence discussion and application, adding verified references rather than repeating definitions. Suggested allocation: introduction 15%, analysis 70%, conclusion 15%.

Paragraph method

Claim → cited evidence → your analysis → specific example → implication for the question.
