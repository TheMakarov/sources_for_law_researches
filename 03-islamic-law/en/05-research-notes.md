## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm the topic fits the instructor’s requirements.
- [ ] Record the actual deadline, word count, and citation style.
- [ ] Read original sources and check citations.
- [ ] Check any legislative amendments affecting the examples used.
- [ ] Distinguish legislation, fiqh, teaching commentary, and empirical evidence.
- [ ] Label hypothetical examples; do not claim to have conducted research you did not conduct.
- [ ] Check Arabic–English terminology; teaching translations are not official legal texts.
- [ ] Add required cover details and remove blanks from the submission copy.
- [ ] Explain the question, conclusion, and two examples without reading.
